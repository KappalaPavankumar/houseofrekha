What Makes Maggam Embroidery Special?

Maggam embroidery is known for its detailed handcrafted appearance and rich decorative finish. The embroidery is created on fabric secured to a frame, allowing intricate patterns and embellishments to be worked with precision.

Zari, stones, beads, pearls, mirrors and embroidery threads can be combined to create different textures and levels of detail. A lighter design can provide an elegant finish for a reception or festive blouse, while heavier embellishment can create a more elaborate bridal appearance.
