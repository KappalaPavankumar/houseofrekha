How to use this

Swap every dashed / labelled block above for your own photography — the layouts, spacing and type are already set up for real photos, this is just a stand-in so you can judge composition before shooting or picking images. Concept 2 is closest to a safe, quick win over your current banner. Concept 1 feels the most premium/bridal but needs one strong hero photo. Concept 3 works well if you have a small library of detail shots already.
