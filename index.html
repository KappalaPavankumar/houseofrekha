Our Story

About Rekha's Aari Arts

Rekha's Aari Arts began as a one-woman hooking needle and a promise: every bridal blouse deserves the same care as the wedding it's made for. Today our small studio of artisans specializes in custom bridal blouses, Maggam work, stone work, Zardosi and fine thread embroidery — each piece personalised to the bride's fabric, silhouette and story.
