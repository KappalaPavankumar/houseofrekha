Our Story

Bridal Blouse Artistry in Madanapalle

Rekha's Aari Arts is a bridal blouse and hand-embroidery studio in Madanapalle, Andhra Pradesh. We create customised designs for weddings, engagements, receptions and festive occasions using Aari, Maggam, Zardosi, stone, cutwork, mirror, thread and zari techniques.

Every design is planned to complement your saree, blouse fabric, preferred motifs, colours and overall bridal look. From detailed blouse backs and sleeves to elegant necklines and borders, each piece is handcrafted with close attention to balance, finish and personal style.
